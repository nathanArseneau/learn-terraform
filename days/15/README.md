## 🔁 Day 15 — Recall 1: Weeks 1 and 2

The first of four spaced-repetition days. Every one is marked 🔁 so you can find them at a glance. The rule on these days is the same: **close the documentation, close your old code, and retrieve from memory first.** Looking something up before you have tried to recall it feels productive and teaches you almost nothing.

**Retrieve (30 minutes, nothing open)**

Write the answers out longhand in `notes/recall-1.md`, then check.

- [ ] Every job `terraform init` does
- [ ] What `validate` catches that `plan` does not, and vice versa
- [ ] Variable precedence, lowest to highest
- [ ] What `~> 3.0` allows that `~> 3.0.1` does not
- [ ] Why `.terraform.lock.hcl` is committed and `.terraform/` is not
- [ ] The difference between a resource and a data source, in one sentence
- [ ] What `count` does badly that `for_each` does well
- [ ] Three jobs the state file performs

**Rebuild (30 minutes)** — From an empty directory, rebuild the Day 7 stack — network, Apache container on 8000, volume, one variable, one output — without opening Day 7. Whatever you have to look up is your weak spot; note it.

**Watch out** — Resist the urge to skip this because it feels like standing still. The material from Weeks 1 and 2 is what you will be examined on most heavily, and it is the oldest by the time you sit the exam.
