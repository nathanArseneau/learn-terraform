### Day 59 — Mock lab 2 (four hours, full dress rehearsal)

The real exam's length. Do it in one sitting, at the time of day you intend to book the exam.

**Get scenarios you have not seen.** You wrote Day 57 yourself, so you knew what was coming. This time, have another LLM generate them: give it the [Advanced exam content list](https://developer.hashicorp.com/terraform/tutorials/pro-cert/adv-review), including its AWS resource list, and ask for four independent lab scenarios of five to seven tasks each, plus ten multiple-choice questions, with answers held back until you ask. Ask for tasks that start from existing configuration, not only from empty directories, since the exam's scenarios often do.

**Grade with the three checks from Day 57**, then compare your time on each scenario to your Day 48 rebuild.

**Watch out** — If you finish with less than twenty minutes spare, you are not ready yet, however many tasks you completed. Four hours goes faster than people expect. Repeat Days 57 to 59 with fresh scenarios before booking.
